Requirement 02 — Apple App Store & Google Play Store

Mobile App Capabilities & Egyptian In-App Payment Experience

Physical goods are exempt from Apple's 30% IAP fee—so you can use Paymob, Kashier, Fawry, Apple Pay, valU & COD directly inside your iOS and Android apps!

APPLE & GOOGLE STORE FEES
$99/yr Apple + $25 Once Google

Total mandatory store developer account fee is just ~6,200 EGP in Year 1 (~4,950 EGP/yr thereafter), with 0% Apple/Google commission on physical retail orders.

NATIVE SDK vs WEBVIEW CHECKOUT
Paymob Flutter/RN SDK Wins

WooCommerce & Medusa 2.0 let you trigger Paymob's Native Flutter/React Native SDK (including 1-tap Apple Pay & Vodafone Cash) inside the app. Shopify forces users out into a slow WebView browser.

APPLE GUIDELINE 4.2 REJECTION RISK
Never Use Basic WebView Wrappers

Apple rejects cheap WebView wrappers of websites. Using Flutter (FluxStore for WooCommerce or Custom Flutter for Medusa) compiles to 100% native ARM code and passes App Store review smoothly.

Requirement 01 — Egyptian Payment Gateways & Couriers

Egyptian Payment Rails Supported Across Web & Mobile Apps

CARDS, MEEZA & APPLE PAY
Paymob / Kashier / PayTabs

Accepts domestic Meeza debit cards, Visa/Mastercard, and Apple Pay directly inside iOS apps and web checkouts (~2.2%–2.75% + 3 EGP).

MOBILE WALLETS & INSTAPAY
Vodafone Cash & InstaPay

Over 40M active Egyptian wallet users (Vodafone Cash, Orange, e& money, WE Pay) via Paymob/Kashier + custom InstaPay IPA/QR screenshot upload.

KIOSK CASH & COD
FawryPay + Bosta / Mylerz

Generates a 10-digit Fawry Reference Code (24–48h stock reservation) or verifies Cash on Delivery (COD) via automated WhatsApp OTP before dispatching Bosta/Mylerz.

BUY NOW, PAY LATER (BNPL)
valU / Sympl / Souhoola

Crucial for orders >1,500 EGP. Merchant receives 100% upfront payout from valU/Sympl/Paymob while the customer pays in 3 to 60 monthly installments.
